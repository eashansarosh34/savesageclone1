The problem

Reward maths is deliberately confusing

Banks make it hard to know what you actually earn. That's the point. Here's what trips everyone up.

🧢

Hidden caps

Your "5% card" quietly stops at ₹1,000/month across all categories combined. Most people never notice they've hit the ceiling.

🚫

Silent exclusions

Rent, fuel, wallet loads, and EMIs often earn nothing: but the card's headline rate never mentions it.

💸

Fees that erase rewards

A ₹2,500 fee + 18% GST can wipe out a year of rewards: unless you hit the waiver threshold, which changes the math entirely.
