What CardIQ does

CardIQ is a decision-support tool for India credit card holders. You tell it how much you spend and where; it tells you which card — or combination of cards — earns the most after fees, caps, and the real value of points.

No bank login. No scraping. No sponsored rankings. All calculations happen in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

How the single-category ranking works

The ranking engine uses Fuzzy TOPSIS — Technique for Order Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution (Chen, 2000). It is a peer-reviewed multi-criteria decision method used in published financial product research.

The match score (0–100)

Each card is evaluated at two extremes: the ideal card (best possible score on every criterion) and the worst card. The match score is the Closeness Coefficient — how close each card is to the ideal, divided by the total range. A score of 82 means this card is 82% of the way from worst to ideal for your specific inputs.

Point and miles conversion

All non-cashback rewards are converted to an equivalent ₹cashback percentage before ranking. Formula: raw rate × ₹per point × 100. You control the redemption assumption (average / business class / economy) in the preferences bar. A card earning "10X reward points" at ₹0.25/point becomes 2.5% effective cashback — the raw multiplier is converted to real rupee value.

How the wallet optimizer works

The optimizer uses exhaustive combinatorial search (Pisinger, 1995; Mansini et al., 2015). Every possible 1, 2, and 3-card combination is evaluated — up to 56 portfolios for 8 cards. This is fast enough to run instantly in a browser.

For each combination, the algorithm:
  1. Assigns each spending category to the card that maximises effective cashback after caps (greedy argmax assignment)
  2. Sums monthly rewards across all categories
  3. Subtracts the combined annual fee (amortised monthly)
  4. Produces a net monthly value in ₹
Minimum spend enforcement

Any card whose minimum monthly spend requirement exceeds your total monthly spend is removed from the eligible pool before optimization begins. A warning is shown so you understand why a card was excluded.

Why the net value is in ₹— not points or percentages

Research on consumer financial decisions consistently shows that people overestimate the value of rewards when they are expressed as points or percentages, and underestimate the cost of annual fees when they are paid yearly rather than monthly (Thaler, 1985; Ausubel, 1991).

CardIQ always displays: (monthly reward − monthly fee cost) × 12 = net annual value in ₹. This is the single number that matters. The raw reward rate is shown too, but it is never the primary figure.

How CardIQ makes money — and why you can trust the rankings

We're a pure-software product with no bank sponsorships and no merchant deals to negotiate. We make money three ways, and none of them bends the advice you get:

1. Subscriptions — Pro ₹499/yr & Pro+ ₹999/yr
Automation and the reconciliation engine. A fraction of what others charge. Everything essential stays free.
2. Referral fees on card applications
If you apply for a card we recommended and get approved, the bank pays us. The ranking is computed before we ever look at referral availability — a card with no referral link still ranks #1 if it's genuinely best for you.
3. Gift-card redemption
When your points are about to expire, we help you convert them into a gift card and earn a small cut. You can always redeem free in your bank app — we just make the reminder and the option convenient.
What we never do: take money to rank a card higher, issue or hold points, move your money, or sell your data. If a recommendation ever seems off, the full math is shown for every number so you can check it yourself.
📱 CardIQ + UPI — built for how India actually pays

81% of UPI-linked credit spend now comes from Tier 2/3 India, and RuPay credit cards earn rewards on UPI transactions that Visa/Mastercard don't. CardIQ models UPI as its own spending category, so you can see which RuPay card actually pays you for everyday UPI payments.

When you know your best UPI card, link it in GPay or PhonePe and pay as usual — CardIQ tells you which card to link, you keep the payment app you already love. We're the intelligence layer, not another payment app to install.
