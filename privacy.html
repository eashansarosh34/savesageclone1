Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At CardIQ, we are committed to protecting your privacy. We believe in complete transparency and data ownership. Unlike traditional financial apps, we do not require you to share your bank credentials or grant unrestricted access to your personal email accounts.

1. Data Collection & Processing

We only collect and process data that is absolutely necessary to provide our credit card recommendation and reconciliation services:

  • Core Calculator: When using our credit card rewards optimizer, all spending calculations are processed locally in your web browser. We do not store or transmit your spending profiles to our servers.
  • Gmail Integration (Pro Option): If you choose to enable the Gmail Auto-Sync feature, we request read-only access to your emails. Our engine only searches for and extracts transactional data from known, verified bank senders. We never read, store, or transmit your personal emails.
  • Statement Auditing: Uploaded bank statements are parsed locally to identify missing reward points. This data is used solely to generate dispute letters and is never stored on our servers.

2. Third-Party Integrations

We partner with secure, industry-standard providers to handle account logins and subscriptions:

  • Supabase: Used to manage secure, encrypted user accounts and preferences for users who opt to save their wallets.
  • Razorpay: Handles all premium subscription transactions securely. We do not store your credit card or payment details.

3. Data Ownership & Deletion

You own your data. You can delete your CardIQ account and all associated transaction records at any time with a single click from your profile settings page.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our support team.
